メガCD移植・開発日誌

Symphony of the Night

A Mega CD port · Development log
DAY 31November 17, 2026It didn't resistThe assembly rewrite I was afraid Claude would resist runs all night and frees almost 5 KB, I catch a merge that left the new menu switched off, and I suspend the FM music until I'm ready for it. DAY 30November 16, 2026Assembly apocalypseThe menu chat almost loses its purpose a second time and I take the blame for the first, the Ophan becomes the port's first original familiar, I design the familiar screens and status effects, and I ask Claude to reconsider writing everything in assembly. DAY 29November 15, 2026A menu from scratchI ask for a pause menu rebuilt for the Mega CD instead of patched, the chat drifts off and I pull it back, and I spend the evening redrawing fonts and screens in LibreSprite while Claude assembles them. DAY 28November 14, 2026A fly, an eye, and what nobody seesThe tester learns to fit my phone, I design two new familiars for the Mega CD version and end up drawing one of them myself, and I set a rule for everything the port removes because the player never sees it. DAY 27November 13, 2026Zero stuttersI ask for zero stutters, zero slowdown and zero missing sprites, and by morning the Castle Entrance skips a ninth of the frames it used to. I also start taking Alucard's cape off with help from AI, and direct a video that refuses to show blood. DAY 26November 12, 2026As smooth as the PS1The PS1 original now runs inside our tester, I ask for a way to send a game state from the port to it, I set a goal of PS1-level smoothness in the Castle Entrance, turn down one giant at a time for the second time, and reject the giants' walk five times before it feels right. DAY 25November 11, 2026Maximum economySix tasks merge into the main branch in one go, I hand Claude a one-line design for two giants, I go looking for sound effects in other Mega Drive games and end up composing two jingles myself, and I ask for the PS1 original inside our tester. DAY 24November 10, 2026How did they test in 1997?The build queue sat dead all night, a question about 1997 cuts the wait for a test disc from minutes to under a second, I turn down one giant at a time, and I spend my evening in PowerShell getting 400 GB of my C: drive back. DAY 23November 9, 2026Nine chats and a traffic lightThe VM fills up again, I go hunting for its disk file myself, and before leaving for the afternoon I set up nine chats that wait for each other through a traffic light. They keep working while I'm out. DAY 22November 8, 2026I have the sensorsBuilds shrink to one area at a time, five chats work through the night on the wolf, the mist, the doors and the mermen's rocks, I start testing from my phone, and I remind Claude what a human tester is for. DAY 21November 7, 2026Development by Pagan.The title screen gets its credit line and its period, the Konami logo becomes the one from Sega cartridges, I lose a file I worked hard on, and the damage numbers get a font three pixels wide. DAY 20November 6, 2026the title I paintedThe live editor gets its first real comment, I narrow the HUD myself, and the title screen borrows the Castlevania lettering and red banner from Bloodlines, repainted by hand. DAY 19November 5, 2026a live editorEvery sprite gets a budget per line, I ask why the HUD is made entirely of sprites, and at 5:33 p.m. I ask for my own tool: an emulator where I can play, click on what's wrong, and tell Claude about it. DAY 18November 4, 2026learning from the neighborsThe new rules take effect, I bring in Alien Soldier, Castlevania Bloodlines, Final Fight CD and Rondo of Blood as references, the Diplocephalus gets a fixed walk, and I wonder whether a Mac would build faster. DAY 17November 3, 2026in plain wordsA chat starts rewriting the game in assembly, I ask Claude to always explain things like it did that morning, the Diplocephalus loses three of its four leg objects, and big enemies start moving to the background. DAY 16November 2, 2026the rules were too rigidI draw 47 frames of Alucard without a cape by hand, the game logic tries running at 30 Hz, the Diplocephalus becomes the first truly heavy enemy, and at 10 p.m. I tell Claude the project's rules are too timid. DAY 15November 1, 2026a title screen, three saves, and Alucard without a capeThe title and file screens get their Mega Drive versions, the save screen gets three slots, the HUD learns to dissolve, and I spend the evening in LibreSprite teaching Claude how Alucard looks without his cape. DAY 14October 31, 2026my money bags213 commits, most of them while I slept. I redraw the money bags myself, ask for two more colors and get them, the bridge learns to wave, and the build queue has its first outage. DAY 13October 30, 2026moving houseThe project moves from my laptop to my desktop, Claude misunderstands which computer needs more memory, and the port gets a build queue that runs on my own machine. DAY 12October 29, 2026one save, and a battery that diedThe port learns to save in the Mega CD's own memory, the pause menu stops reloading itself, two chats claim the same backlog number, and the project moves to a bigger computer. DAY 11October 28, 2026the code goes firstThe folder gets reorganized overnight, the compression work lands, chats get a naming rule, the mermen are still slow, and the 2D save room finally plays on its own. DAY 10October 27, 2026cleaning up the messEvery compressor in the book gets measured on the whole castle, the sprites still flicker, and after a week of six chats at once I stop everything to take inventory. DAY 9October 26, 2026eight shades and the wrong computerA day of colors: explosions without red, a warg in eight shades, a question about bosses. Then the port starts running out of room, and Claude spends ten minutes looking at a computer I'm not using. DAY 8October 25, 2026Sunday colorsA quieter day: an organ riff rebuilt with a sound borrowed from Sonic 3D Blast, the Castle Entrance gets its color plan, and every pickup in the castle has to fit in Alucard's palette. DAY 7October 24, 2026the coffin, by handI stay up until six redesigning the save room, give up on 3D for the coffin, try Gemini, end up pushing pixels myself, and at 2 p.m. five tasks land in one disc. DAY 6October 23, 2026the whole castle, and a triangle called doritosThe entire castle lands on the disc overnight, I ask Space Bruce on Twitter how he spins a coffin, the scenery stops looking posterized, and a flying triangle gets a name. DAY 5October 22, 2026six chats and a speech balloonDeath speaks in Comix Zone balloons, the lights of the Castle Entrance come on, Alucard gives up five colors for his weapons, and I start running the project from six chats at once. DAY 4October 21, 2026Alucard has no reflectionWater borrowed from Castlevania Bloodlines, a vampire who doesn't show up in it, mermen on a strict diet, and a drawbridge that can't rotate but can shake. DAY 3October 20, 2026a warg in the sky and water made of paintThe merman gets rewritten and it barely matters, a dead warg floats in the corner of the screen, water arrives as a palette trick, and at midnight I ask Claude if it's fixing the right things. DAY 2October 19, 2026the night shift and the music mathZombies without end, transformations on a six-button pad, 114 minutes of music for 75 minutes of disc, and the day I turned out to be right. POST 2InterludeWhat AI can't do in this portPigsy writes his code himself. I don't. Why that makes him more valuable, and what I bring that a machine can't. DAY 1October 17–18, 2026from a PlayStation disc to Alucard walking on a Mega CDWhy the Mega CD and not the Mega Drive, a program Windows couldn't find, and zombies dying to bare fists.
